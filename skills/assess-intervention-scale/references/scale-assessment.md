# Scale Assessment

## Five voltage risks

### 1. False positive

- Was the hypothesis and primary outcome declared before analysis?
- Was the study powered for a practically meaningful effect?
- Are assignment, attrition, exclusions, instrumentation, and multiple testing trustworthy?
- Has the finding replicated or survived an independent reanalysis?
- Is the published result likely to overrepresent large positive effects?

### 2. Audience mismatch

- Compare early adopters with ordinary and hard-to-reach users.
- Compare baseline motivation, ability, opportunity, trust, literacy, language, culture, and vulnerability.
- Identify segments for whom the mechanism could reverse.

### 3. Unscalable ingredients

- Separate the replicable recipe from the exceptional chef.
- Inventory training, supervision, expertise, relationships, novelty, manual work, and fidelity controls.
- Ask whether normal operators can deliver the intervention under normal load.

### 4. Spillovers

- Capacity and congestion
- Peer and norm effects
- Substitution to another channel or harmful behavior
- Strategic response, fraud, and gaming
- Competitor, supplier, employee, household, or community response
- General-equilibrium changes once the intervention is common

### 5. Cost trap

- Fixed versus marginal cost
- Acquisition and support costs for later users
- Quality-control and fraud cost
- Infrastructure, compliance, localization, and exception handling
- Cost per durable user outcome, not cost per exposure or click
