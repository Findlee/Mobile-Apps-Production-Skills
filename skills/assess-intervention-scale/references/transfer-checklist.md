## Last-yard checks

- Can the intended user notice and understand the intervention in the real channel?
- Can they perform the action immediately with local resources and permissions?
- Does delivery work during peak load, interruption, error, and low connectivity?
- Can normal staff or systems deliver it without exceptional supervision?
- Are language, units, norms, and examples locally meaningful?
- Is the intervention acceptable and proportionate, or does it feel invasive or creepy?
- Are refusal, reversal, support, and recovery available?
- Are exposure, fidelity, mechanism, outcome, and harm measurable?
