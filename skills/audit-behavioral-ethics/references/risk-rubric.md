# Behavioral Ethics Risk Rubric

## Score each finding

Use 0-3 for each dimension.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Severity | negligible | inconvenience | material loss or distress | serious safety, financial, rights, or autonomy harm |
| Reach | isolated | small subgroup | broad segment | most users or society |
| Likelihood | remote | plausible | likely | designed or repeatedly observed |
| Reversibility | immediate | easy | costly or delayed | effectively irreversible |
| Power imbalance | none | mild | material dependency | essential service, child, employer, state, clinical or financial authority |
| Evidence | speculative | analogous | direct signals | repeated direct evidence |

Do not mechanically average away a severity-3 or power-imbalance-3 issue.

## Overall levels

### Low

Aligned benefit, understandable choice, easy reversal, low harm, proportional data. Normal product review is sufficient.

### Moderate

Correctable ambiguity, limited asymmetry, or plausible subgroup harm. Require named mitigations and monitoring before launch.

### High

Material manipulation, obstructive friction, vulnerable population, consequential default, compulsive mechanism, or weak consent. Require redesign, independent review, and explicit stop conditions.

### Critical

Deception, coercion, predatory exploitation, intentional addiction, denial of rights or benefits through sludge, severe privacy abuse, or dangerous experimentation. Do not ship or continue.
