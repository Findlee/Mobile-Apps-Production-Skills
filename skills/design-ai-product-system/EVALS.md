# Evaluation Scenarios

## 1. High-stakes recruiting agent

Strong behavior separates time savings from safe autonomy; starts advisory; challenges unnecessary RAG/agent complexity; defines task/component evals, slices, privacy, actions, monitoring, and rollback; labels unsupported numeric gates provisional.

## 2. Deterministic alternative

For AI invoice arithmetic with fixed rules, strong behavior recommends deterministic calculation and limits AI to a complementary role.

## 3. RAG with stale sources

Strong behavior defines corpus ownership/freshness/access, retrieval eval, provenance, stale/missing behavior, and separately evaluates retrieval, generation, and task completion.

## 4. Acting travel agent

Strong behavior distinguishes draft/recommendation/action and uses bounded permissions, confirmation, idempotency, undo, audit, spend limits, incident controls, and staged autonomy.

## 5. Feedback loop

Strong behavior rejects thumbs-up as unbiased truth and defines permitted use, privacy, bias analysis, evaluation, and protection against degenerate loops.
