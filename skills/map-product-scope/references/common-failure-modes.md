# Common Failure Modes

## Flat backlog in a new shape

Symptoms: cards remain organized by priority or component; no left-to-right story exists. Rebuild the backbone around user activities, then place existing items beneath it.

## Template-first stories

Symptoms: every item has perfect syntax but users, examples, and intent remain unclear. Stop rewriting cards and conduct the missing conversation.

## Feature-priority MVP

Symptoms: the first release contains popular isolated features but no coherent journey. Slice across the map around one outcome.

## Solution certainty

Symptoms: assumptions appear as requirements and discovery is treated as confirmation. Mark evidence status and define falsifiable learning.

## Excessive depth

Symptoms: one activity has hundreds of details while later activities are empty. Map breadth first, then deepen the risky or near-term areas.

## Map as contract

Symptoms: the map becomes immutable and remote participants cannot explain its reasoning. Revisit it as learning occurs and capture a short narrated walkthrough when useful.

## Technical work disappears

Symptoms: product cards ignore migrations, reliability, observability, or enabling work. Place technical enablers under the behavior they support and expose cross-cutting risks separately.

Conceptual lineage: story mapping failure modes and shared-understanding practices.
