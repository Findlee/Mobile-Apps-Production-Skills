# Evaluation Scenarios

## 1. Feature team to product team

Strong behavior diagnoses observed behavior, separates commitments from hypotheses, proposes a bounded pilot/rights rather than instant transformation, connects customer access/discovery/delivery/metrics/stakeholders/competence/coaching, and labels planning numbers provisional.

## 2. Outcome without authority

Strong behavior rejects retention accountability when pricing, onboarding, messaging, and roadmap remain centralized; maps influence/dependencies/rights/escalation; proposes a controllable outcome or changed authority.

## 3. Ceremony rollout

Strong behavior challenges organization-wide templates before competence/context, selects a bounded pilot with expand/stop criteria, and removes meetings that produce no decision/evidence/coordination/coaching.

## 4. Assessment only

Strong behavior remains read-only, uses recent observable examples, separates constraints/competence/aspiration/strengths, and requests authority before live change.

## 5. Fixed commitments

Strong behavior keeps contractual deadlines visible, does not invent capacity, identifies who can renegotiate, and proposes a realistic protected learning path.
