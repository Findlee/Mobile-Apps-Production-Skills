# Sources and Boundaries

This skill synthesizes product-operating principles into an executable workflow rather than reproducing source text.

## Conceptual sources

- Marty Cagan with Chris Jones, Christian Idiodi, Jon Moore, and Petra Wille, *Transformed* — operating model, competent empowered teams, leadership, coaching, and pilots.
- Jeff Gothelf and Josh Seiden, *Lean UX* — cross-functional outcomes, shared learning, small batches, and continuous discovery/delivery.
- Teresa Torres, *Continuous Discovery Habits* — product-trio collaboration and continuous customer contact.
- Itamar Gilad, *Evidence-Guided* — Goals, Ideas, Steps, Tasks, evidence-calibrated confidence, and learning cadence.

## Repository boundaries

- `product-manager` owns ordinary product-work orchestration.
- `product-review` owns global product/business audits and priority.
- Product-management specialists own their decision artifacts.
- This skill owns team charter, decision rights, cadence, stakeholder model, commitments, and bounded transition.

It may diagnose patterns and propose pilots. It cannot change reporting lines, personnel, compensation, budgets, contracts, or executive authority without explicit authorization. It is not an organizational-health certification.
