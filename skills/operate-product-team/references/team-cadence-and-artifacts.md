# Team Cadence and Artifacts

## Contents

1. Work layers
2. Board
3. Review
4. Step planning
5. Communication

## 1. Work layers

Keep goals, ideas, steps, and tasks distinct. Tasks link upward; detailed tasks do not substitute for shared understanding.

## 2. Board

| Goal | Evidence/baseline | Active ideas | Next steps | Owner/status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Keep rejected, parked, and untriaged ideas in an idea bank. Limit active work. Make the board visible.

## 3. Review

At an agreed interval: review outcome/evidence, reassess bets, keep steps active until analyzed, update impact/effort/confidence/risk, add/remove/stop/reorder, and link delivery planning to chosen steps. Do not turn it into task status.

## 4. Step planning

Define assumption/value increment, actor, method/artifact, success/failure/ambiguity, guardrails, participants/decision owner, timebox/analysis owner, and production cleanup. Planning numbers are provisional unless derived from capacity, method, risk, or accountable decision.

## 5. Communication

Communicate outcome/rationale, evidence gained or contradicted, changed decisions, risks/dependencies, decision points, and release results. Provide challengeable context without freezing assumptions into oversized requirements.
