# Product Analytics Review Reference

Reviewed: 2026-08-08
Primary source: Amplitude North Star and Retention materials, supplemented by the package product-metrics skill.

## North Star Framework

A useful North Star Metric should:

- represent customer value;
- be influenceable by product/marketing;
- be a leading indicator of revenue/business value.

The framework also uses actionable inputs that help explain and drive the North Star.

Do not force a North Star into every feature review.

## Activation

Activation should represent first meaningful value or a validated leading behavior, not simply:

- account created;
- onboarding completed;
- permission granted;
- tutorial viewed.

Review both:

- activation rate;
- time-to-activation;
- activation quality / later retention relationship.

## Retention

Define:

1. Critical event - what recurring action indicates real value?
2. Natural usage interval - daily, weekly, monthly, or episodic?
3. Retention method appropriate to the usage pattern.

Amplitude's retention guidance recommends measuring return to a meaningful critical event, not merely app open.

## Lifecycle

Separate when useful:

- New users;
- Current users;
- Resurrected users;
- Dormant/churned users.

An aggregate retention curve can hide materially different lifecycle problems.

## Product-review usage

Use analytics to identify:

- earliest bottleneck;
- cohort differences;
- behavior associated with later value;
- activation/retention segments;
- monetization/retention tradeoffs;
- unexpected regressions.

Do not infer causality from correlation alone.
