## Coverage narrative

Describe areas, states, data, sequences, platforms, and observation channels actually explored. Explain major departures from the charter.

## Findings

| Type | Finding | Evidence | Impact/confidence | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Defect / question / risk / opportunity | ... | ... | ... | ... |

For defect candidates, include minimal reproduction, expected and actual behavior, frequency, environment, side effects, and evidence location.

## Session accounting

Optionally record approximate time spent on setup, testing and design, defect investigation, and reporting. Use it to explain constraints, not to score the tester.

## Coverage gaps and residual uncertainty

List important areas not explored, unreliable evidence, unavailable configurations, and unresolved oracle questions.

## Recommended next charters

Prioritize follow-up charters by new risk or uncertainty. Keep each charter focused on one information goal.
